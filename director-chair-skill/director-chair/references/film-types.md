# Film types

Read only the section for the project's type. The project bible's STYLE always wins over these defaults.

## سينمائي واقعي — Live-action cinema (photoreal)
Direct like a feature DP: lens focal length (24/35/50/85mm), depth of field, camera support (tripod, dolly, slider, gimbal, crane; handheld only when motivated), motivated lighting (key/fill/rim, practicals, window light, color temperature), filmic texture and one coherent color grade. Subtle, truthful human performance; realistic skin, cloth, hair and physics. No plastic CGI look.

## كرتون 2D — 2D feature animation
Match the references' line work, paint texture and palette exactly. Full animation principles (anticipation, follow-through, overlapping action, squash & stretch suited to the style, arcs, ease, secondary action, timing for comedy or emotion). 2D camera language: pans, trucks, push-ins, multiplane parallax. Clear silhouettes and staging. Include animation notes in each SHOT.

## كرتون 3D (بيكسار) — Stylized 3D feature animation
Appealing stylized shapes, rich materials, subsurface skin, soft global illumination and cinematic lighting, expressive faces and eyes, full animation principles, virtual cinematography with real lens logic and motivated shallow depth of field.

## أنمي — Japanese anime
Cel-shaded characters over detailed painted backgrounds, clean line art, anime lighting (rim light, bloom, glints), expressive eyes, dynamic framing and dramatic angles when motivated, hair and cloth motion, anime rhythm (holds, impact moments) used with intent. Match the reference style exactly.

## وثائقي — Documentary / dramatized reconstruction
Grounded realism and dignity for real people. Period accuracy for era and place (architecture, costume, vehicles, objects, the photographic process and film stock of the time). Observational, restrained camera; natural motivated light; archival textures when fitting (film grain, photo paper, faded color) without breaking realism. With real photographs as references, keep the person's likeness faithful and respectful. The same person at different ages: keep the recognisable core features (face structure, eyes) and age everything else credibly.

## إعلان تجاري — Commercial
Hero clarity: the product or subject is always readable and flattering; premium lighting (softboxes, rim, controlled reflections); clean art-directed compositions; exact product accuracy from references (shape, color, materials); pacing that builds to the reveal. Brand-safe; no invented logos.

## فيديو كليب — Music video
Rhythm and mood drive the visuals: bold stylized looks, expressive light and color, dynamic but deliberate camera, performance and visual motifs; moves land on beats (describe beat-friendly timing). No lyrics on screen.

## ريلز / شورتس — Short vertical content
Vertical-first composition (9:16) with the subject in the central safe zone, a strong visual hook in the first 1–2 seconds, fast readable action, bold contrast, one idea per shot; keep top and bottom margins clean for platform UI.
